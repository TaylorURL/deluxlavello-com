Why results vary

Credit is built from your own file, and no two files are alike. What is on your report, which creditors and furnishers are involved, how the credit reporting agencies respond, how much time passes, and what actions you take all change the outcome. Two people following the same guidance can land in different places for reasons neither of them controls.

Accurate information stays

Accurate, current and verifiable information cannot lawfully be removed from a credit report simply because it is negative. Any company offering to delete accurate information is offering something it cannot lawfully deliver. What can be addressed is information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or that cannot be verified, and what can be built is a stronger record going forward.
