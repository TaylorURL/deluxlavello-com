Preparation

What to have ready

Nothing is required for a first conversation. Copies of the credit reports help if they are already to hand; the consultation covers how to pull all three at no cost otherwise. A debt lawsuit needs the summons and any letters from the collector, along with the date each one arrived.

Response times

A matter carrying a court deadline moves to the front when the first line says so.

What the first conversation covers

Roughly thirty minutes: where the credit file stands now, what is holding it back, and which of the four paths fits. It ends with a plain statement of what the work would involve and how long it would take. There is no charge for it and no obligation afterwards.
