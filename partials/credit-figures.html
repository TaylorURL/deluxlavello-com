What Shapes a Score

FICO publishes how much weight each part of your file carries.

  • Payment History35%

    Whether you pay on time, every account, every month.

  • Amounts Owed30%

    How much of your available credit you are using right now.

  • Length of History15%

    How long your accounts have been open, oldest and average.

  • New Credit10%

    Recent applications and the inquiries they leave behind.

  • Credit Mix10%

    Revolving accounts like cards alongside installment loans.
