Privacy

Privacy policy

What Delux L Avello LLC collects, what it is used for, who it reaches, and what you can ask the company to do with it.

Information we collect

Depending on how you reach the company, that may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Information you send through any form published on this website
  • What you tell us when you request or attend a consultation
  • Device, browser, network address and similar technical information the website records
  • Anything else you choose to provide

What this website itself records is narrow, and it is worth being exact about. There is no contact form, no newsletter signup, and no login on this site, so no page here asks you to type anything. Two things happen in the background. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs: the address requested, the time, the browser identification string, and the network address the request came from. And when a page fails, the browser sends a fault report so the fault can be found and fixed, carrying the error text, the address of the page, and the browser identification string. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking pixels. Typefaces are served from this site rather than a third-party font host, so opening a page reaches no other company.

Identification documents, credit reports and the rest of the sensitive information that enrollment requires are handled through the secure client and onboarding systems, after a written agreement is in place. They are not requested through ordinary public website forms, and no page on this site should ever be used to send a Social Security number, an account number, or a credit report.

How we use information

Information is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Schedule consultations
  • Provide the services you ask for
  • Complete onboarding
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain business and compliance records
  • Improve the website and the services
  • Prevent fraud and security incidents
  • Meet legal obligations that apply to the company

Service providers

Running the company takes outside tools, and information reaches them where it is reasonably necessary to operate. Those are providers of the kind you would expect: customer relationship management, scheduling, communications, payment processing, document and electronic signature, client management, and credit monitoring. They receive what their part of the work requires and are expected to handle it accordingly.

Delux L Avello LLC does not sell personal information. It is not rented or traded either, and it is not shared with anyone outside the company and its service providers except where the law requires it.

Your privacy rights

You can ask what information the company holds about you, ask that it be corrected, and ask that it be deleted. You can ask for a copy of it, and you can ask the company to stop contacting you. Where the law gives you a further right over your information, you have that right here too, and exercising any of them costs you nothing and changes nothing about the service you receive.

Send the request to support@deluxlavello.com. Say what you are asking for and give enough detail to find your records. You may be asked to confirm your identity first, which is there to stop someone else asking about you.

Security

The company uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No transmission over the internet and no electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, and any company telling you otherwise is overstating what is possible.

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected for, and for as long as legal, accounting, contractual and compliance obligations require. When neither applies any longer, it is disposed of.

Children

This site and these services are meant for adults making decisions about their own credit. They are not directed to children under 13, and information is not knowingly collected from them.

This policy changes when the practices behind it change or when the law requires it. The date below is the version you are reading.
