The Work

How the two halves connect

Building and growth are the same work at different stages. Building puts positive history on a file that does not have enough of it. Growth shapes a file that does. Taking both as one path means the handover never happens: the guide who sets the foundation is the one adjusting it once it is established.

In practice the early months look like the building path, focused on payment history and account management. As the file matures, the emphasis moves to utilization, mix, and preparation for whatever comes next. Nothing restarts at the boundary, because there is no boundary to cross.

Who this suits

People who know credit is going to matter for something specific a year or two out, and would rather build toward it deliberately than repair it in a hurry. It also suits anyone starting over who wants one relationship for the whole climb rather than a series of them.
