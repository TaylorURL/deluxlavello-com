The Work

What optimization actually means

Growth work starts from a file that already has history in it. The review looks at utilization on each card and across all of them, the age of the accounts, the mix of revolving and installment credit, and how recent applications have shaped the picture.

From there the adjustments are specific: which balance to bring down first, which account to leave open even though it goes unused, when a limit increase helps and when requesting one costs more than it returns. Each change is explained, so the reasoning outlasts the plan.

Working toward a specific goal

A mortgage, a vehicle, or a business line each reads a file differently, and each has its own timeline. A goal and its date turn the plan into a schedule, built backward from the decision, with the steps that matter to that lender placed where they have time to report.
