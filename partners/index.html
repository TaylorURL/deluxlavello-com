Referrals

How a referral works

A referral is a name and a reason. From there it is a consultation, an honest read of where the file stands, and a plan with a timeline attached. The referring business hears when the customer is ready to come back, and nothing else about the file.

There is no portal to learn and no volume commitment. A phone number or an email introduction is enough, and the follow-up runs on this side.

What the customer experiences

The same consultation every client gets, with the referral noted so the introduction is not repeated back to them as a sales call. Nothing is charged for the consultation, and nobody is enrolled in a path they did not choose.

Who partners with us

Auto dealers and finance managers, mortgage originators, realtors, insurance agents, and any business that watches good customers get declined for one reason. Where a credit decision is the last step before a sale, a referral turns a dead end into a customer with a date on the calendar.
