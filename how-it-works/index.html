The Process

Four steps

  1. Schedule a Consultation

    Virtual or mobile in-person, by appointment. We talk through your situation, your goals, and which path fits: Credit Building, Credit Growth, both, or lawsuit support.

  2. Review Your Credit Picture

    We walk through your credit reports and history together, in plain language, so you know exactly where you stand before anything else happens.

  3. Follow Your Plan

    You get a clear, personalized plan for your path, with education built into every step, so you understand each move and can keep the habits for life.

  4. Keep Building

    Credit develops over time. As yours does, the guidance continues: checkpoints, adjustments, and straight answers whenever you need them.

Expectations

What we will and will not tell you

We will tell you what your reports say, what your options are, and what the trade-offs look like. We will teach you how credit works so the progress belongs to you.

We will not promise a specific score increase, guarantee an approval, or claim accurate information can be removed from your reports. No honest company can, and honest is the only way we work.

The Rhythm

What happens between sessions

Most of the work happens away from the sessions, and most of it is small: paying on the date the plan names, keeping a balance under the line it sets, leaving an account open that was ready to be closed. The sessions exist to read what the reports say back and adjust from there.

Between them the plan is in writing, and a question can be asked when something unexpected arrives. A pre-approval letter or a limit change is easier to answer in the week it lands than at the next review.

How long it takes

Lenders report on monthly cycles, so the earliest measurable change is thirty to sixty days out. A thin file usually shows meaningful movement across six to twelve months. A file carrying recent negative marks takes longer, because time is one of the inputs and nothing shortens it.
