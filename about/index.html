How we work

Every engagement starts with a consultation rather than a program. The reports come first, the plan comes second, and the plan names what the client will do rather than what will be done to them. That order is the whole method: a person who understands why a step works keeps the result after the engagement ends.

Some work gets turned down. Accurate information stays on a report, scores are not promised by the point, and a situation that calls for a lawyer gets told so.
